What TechBridge is

TechBridge is the training arm of Baselink Services Limited, built for people who already know a little and want to know enough — enough to build, to ship, to be useful on day one of a real job.

Instead of long theory-heavy courses, TechBridge runs short, practical programs, then hands you a live, structured internship to put what you learned to work. You leave with something you built, not just something you watched.

Who it's for

Students, graduates and career-switchers who want practical, job-ready tech skills — not just certificates.

How it works

Pick a track, learn the fundamentals, then apply them inside a guided 30-day internship on real project work.

Why it matters

The gap between "I learned it" and "I can do it" is where most people get stuck. TechBridge is built to close that gap.

The internship

A 30-day practical internship

Once you've got the fundamentals down, TechBridge moves you into a 30-day internship built around real, hands-on project work — not busywork, not filler.

You'll be working the way teams actually work: guided, project-based, and focused on producing something you can point to afterward.

  • Structured, project-based work over 30 days
  • Direct application of what you learned in the program
  • Possibility of a recommendation letter, based on your performance
